|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Operational Thermodynamics: Brayton Cycle Foundation
The turbine functions according to the open Brayton cycle:
1. **Compression (1 $\to$ 2):** Atmospheric air at $(AT, AP)$ is drawn through filters $(\Delta P = AFDP)$ and compressed isentropically to high discharge pressure $(CDP)$.
2. **Combustion (2 $\to$ 3):** Fuel (natural gas) is mixed with compressed air and combusted at constant pressure, raising gas temperature to Turbine Inlet Temperature $(TIT)$.
3. **Expansion (3 $\to$ 4):** Superheated gas expands through turbine stages driving the compressor shaft and the electrical generator, producing Turbine Energy Yield $(TEY)$, before discharging at Turbine After Temperature $(TAT)$ and Exhaust Pressure $(GTEP)$.

---

## 5. Dual Prediction Track Isolation
- **Track A (Emissions Focus):** Models $f_A(AT, AP, AH, AFDP, GTEP, TIT, TAT, TEY, CDP) \to (CO, NOX)$.
- **Track B (Joint Emissions & Yield Focus):** Models $f_B(AT, AP, AH, AFDP, GTEP, TIT, TAT, CDP) \to (CO, NOX, TEY)$.
- **Strict Constraint:** $TEY$ is physically withheld from all feature matrices in Track B to preserve validity for pre-dispatch planning.
